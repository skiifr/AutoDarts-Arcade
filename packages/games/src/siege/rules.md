# Siege

Hit the numbers the invaders carry before they reach your wall. 2–8 players.

*Party prototype: these are the rules only; art and stage come in Phase 5.*

## Setup

- Everyone defends their own **wall of 10 bricks**.
- At the start of every round a **wave of invaders** appears **3 steps** from every
  wall. Everyone faces the same wave: the same numbers and the same strengths.
- Each invader carries a board number from 1 to 20 and has a **strength** (1 or 2).

## Your turn

Throw three darts at **your own** invaders.

- Hit an invader's number and you damage the **closest** invader carrying it: a single
  does 1 damage, a double 2 and a treble 3. At zero strength it is destroyed. Extra
  damage is lost.
- The **bull hits every one of your invaders** at once: 1 damage for the outer 25, 2 for
  the bull.
- Any other dart does nothing.

## End of the round

When everyone has thrown, every invader **steps forward**. An invader that reaches your
wall knocks off **one brick for each point of strength it has left**, and is gone.

If your wall falls to **zero**, you are out. Then the next wave appears.

## Winning

- The **last player standing** wins.
- Otherwise, after the last round (10 by default) the **strongest wall** wins. Level
  walls go to whoever destroyed more invaders; if that is level too, the place is shared.
- Players who are out rank below everyone still standing, by how long they lasted, then
  by invaders destroyed.

## Difficulty

| Difficulty | Invaders per wave | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 2 | 1 |
| Normal (default) | 2 | 1–2 |
| Hard | 3 | 1–2 |
