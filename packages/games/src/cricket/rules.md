# Cricket

Close 15 to 20 and the bull, and score on whatever is still open. 2–4 players.

## The numbers

Only **20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15 and the bull** count. Every other number, and a miss, does
nothing.

## Marks

- A single is 1 mark, a double 2 and a treble 3.
- The outer bull (25) is 1 mark and the bull (50) is 2.
- **Three marks close a number** for you.

## Points

Once you have closed a number, every extra mark on it scores that number's value (the
bull is worth 25 a mark), as long as **at least one opponent has not closed it yet**.
When everyone has closed a number it is dead and scores nothing.

A dart can do both: with two marks on 20, a treble 20 closes it with one mark and scores
40 with the other two.

## Winning

You win the moment you have **closed all seven** and you are **not behind** on points
(level is enough).

## Cut-throat (optional)

Your extra marks give points to **every opponent who has not closed that number**, and
your own score does not change. You win when you have closed everything and have **no
more points than anyone else**. Lowest score is best.

## Round limit

If nobody has won after the round limit (20 rounds by default), the game ends once the
round is complete. The best score wins (highest, or lowest in cut-throat). If scores
are level, more marks on the board ranks first; if that is level too, the place is
shared.
