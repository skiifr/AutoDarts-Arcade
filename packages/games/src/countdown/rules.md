# Countdown

Classic x01: race from your start score down to exactly zero. 1–8 players.

## How to play

1. Everyone starts on the same score (301, 501 or 701; 501 by default).
2. Take turns throwing three darts. Each dart's points come off your score: a treble
   is worth three times the number, a double twice, the outer bull 25 and the bull 50.
3. The first player to reach **exactly zero** wins straight away.

## Finishing

- **Double out** (on by default): your last dart must be a double or the bull.
- **Single out**: any dart that reaches zero finishes.

## Bust

Your turn ends at once, and your score goes back to what it was at the start of the
turn, if a dart:

- takes you **below zero**;
- leaves you on **exactly 1** with double out (1 can never be finished);
- reaches **zero without a double** with double out.

Any darts you throw after a bust do not count.

## Double in (optional, off by default)

Your darts only start counting once you hit a double or the bull. That dart counts, and
so does every dart after it.

## Round limit

If nobody has finished after the round limit (20 rounds by default), the game ends once
the round is complete, so everyone has thrown the same number of turns. The lowest
score wins. If two players are level, the one with the higher best turn ranks first; if
that is level too, they share the place.

## Big turns

Score 100 or more with three darts and the board celebrates (bigger for 140+ and 180).
