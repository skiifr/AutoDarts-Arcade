# Around the Clock

Hit 1 through 20 in order, then finish on the bull. 1–8 players.

## How to play

1. Everyone starts on **1**.
2. Take turns throwing three darts. Hit your current number (single, double or treble
   all count) to move on to the next one.
3. After 20, hit the **bull** (the outer 25 or the inner bull) to finish.
4. The first player to finish wins straight away.

## Options

- **Finish on the bull** (on by default). Turn it off and hitting 20 finishes.
- **Doubles and trebles skip ahead** (off by default). A double moves you on two numbers
  and a treble three. You can skip onto the bull but never past it: the bull must still
  be hit. Without the bull, a skip past 20 finishes.

## Round limit

If nobody has finished after the round limit (20 rounds by default), the game ends once
the round is complete and the player furthest round the clock wins. Players on the same
number share the place.
